Interpretting the ICER: when the intervention leads to health benefits (i.e., the difference in QALYs is positive), then an ICER of less than ~$30,000 per QALY is considered cost-effective, and a negative ICER means the intervention is cost-saving.

Abbreviations:
  • HF - heart failure
  • CVD - cardiovascular disease
  • CKD - chronic kidney disease
  • PN - peripheral neuropathy
  • QALYs - quality-adjusted life years
  • ICER - incremental cost-effectiveness ratio
  • IRSD - index of relative socioeconomic disadvantage (higher = less disadvantaged)
